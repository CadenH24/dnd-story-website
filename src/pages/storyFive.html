One thing about Dungeons and Dragons (also known as D&D) has been used as a creative expression, D&D has been used as a form of art, D&D has been used as a storytelling format, D&D has been used as a form of therapy, D&D has been used as a way for people to connect and meet new people.

Every day people listen to podcasts and watch shows surrounded by D&D but people are always looking for a way to talk to people to play games, and D&D provides that with its expansive and creative fan base.

D&D has become a staple with people who are trying to find themselves in a new light and who want to get out of something.

[quote on dealing with depression and Anxiety]

The community that surrounds D&D has helped people with both mental and physical problems, in 2020 Paradox Comics-n-Cards hosted an event for people to donate to 4 Luv of Dog Rescue through D&D having to pay $20 to enter and any rerolls they wanted to do they could pay an extra dollar, all funds were donated and they raised $2,300, $300 over their original goal.

D&D helps people with an outlet and one of these is escapism, D&D has been a way for people to immerse themselves in a world completely made up but also dealing with problems either with themselves or the world they live in.

Like Torres people who have troubles with their depression find D&D to vent and escape.

“(D&D) allows me to be something I’m not” Torres said

Table top role-playing games (ttrpgs) have also been a way for people to express themselves in many of different ways, in a world where being in a minority is hard for some, they look to table top games to express themselves.

D&D not only has made an impact on minorities but also it has been shown that is helps people with their mental health a study was done in 2024, and it showed that people who played D&D experienced more relief especially if they worked a 9 to 5 job.
